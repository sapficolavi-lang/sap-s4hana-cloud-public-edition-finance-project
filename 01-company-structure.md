## 4. Finance Business Areas

### General Ledger (GL)
Purpose: Record and report the company's financial transactions.

Example activities:
- Record expenses and revenue.
- Post journal entries.
- Review account balances.
- Support period-end closing.

### Accounts Payable (AP)
Purpose: Manage amounts owed to suppliers.

Example activities:
- Record supplier invoices.
- Process supplier payments.
- Review outstanding supplier balances.

### Accounts Receivable (AR)
Purpose: Manage amounts customers owe the company.

Example activities:
- Record customer invoices.
- Record incoming payments.
- Review outstanding customer balances.

### Asset Accounting
Purpose: Track company-owned assets and their accounting values.

Example activities:
- Record an asset acquisition.
- Understand depreciation.
- Review asset values.

## 5. Example Business Scenario

GlobalTech Solutions Ltd. purchases office laptops from a supplier for INR 100,000.

The learning exercise will explore:
1. How a supplier invoice is represented in accounting.
2. Which general ledger accounts may be affected.
3. How the supplier liability is recorded.
4. How payment settles the outstanding liability.

Tax treatment and the exact accounts will depend on the scenario assumptions and applicable accounting requirements.
