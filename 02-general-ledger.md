# General Ledger - Practice 01

## Project
ABC India Pvt. Ltd. - SAP S/4HANA Finance Learning Portfolio

## Business Scenario
The company receives ₹50,000 as capital from its owner. The amount is deposited directly into the company bank account.

## Journal Entry

| Account | Debit (INR) | Credit (INR) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bank Account | 50,000 | 0 |
| Owner's Capital | 0 | 50,000 |
| **Total** | **50,000** | **50,000** |

## Accounting Explanation
- Bank is debited because the company's bank asset increases.
- Owner's Capital is credited because the owner's equity increases.
- Total debits equal total credits.

## SAP Learning Objective
Understand the debit and credit logic of a basic General Ledger transaction and identify the corresponding accounts in SAP S/4HANA Finance.
